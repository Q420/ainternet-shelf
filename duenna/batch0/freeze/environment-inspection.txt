== addendum (same sitting): loopback inside the namespace ==
In the plain 'unshare -n' run above, raising loopback was denied (RTNETLINK: Operation not permitted) and lo stayed DOWN: that namespace had no routes and no usable interface at all. With a combined user+network namespace the operator can raise loopback:

exit: 0
