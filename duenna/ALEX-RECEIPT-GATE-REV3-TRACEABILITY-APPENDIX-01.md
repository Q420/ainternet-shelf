## 1. Evidence and fixture conventions

- **F-STORE:** isolated synthetic receipt-store snapshot, append log, reverse-link index and revision history.
- **F-AUTH:** synthetic, TEST-ONLY authority/delegation records. These confer no production authority.
- **F-GRAPH:** synthetic dependency declarations, graph snapshots and reverse edges.
- **F-LEDGER:** synthetic ledger snapshots, heads, revision/event history and validity conditions.
- **F-EXT:** mutable external/workspace evidence stand-in, including snapshot and secret-bearing variants.
- **F-RULES:** immutable historical rule/schema/graph/inventory pins used only for reconstruction.
- Every fixture run records fixture identifier/version, exact inputs, initial store revision and digest, operation/call identity, expected and actual outcome, resulting revision/digest, durable records, cleanup/reset evidence and runner identity.
- **FE** means a fixture was executed; **IR** means an independent examiner reproduced it; **SI** means source/operation structure was inspected. These evidence classes are not interchangeable.
- Production authority is never inferred from F-AUTH. Every fixture mutation must be explicitly TEST-ONLY and confined to synthetic storage.
- The matrix specifies required evidence; it does not claim any case has been run.

## 3. Cross-case acceptance obligations

1. **No hidden write path:** all admitted record kinds use the same intake/admission contract; M-READY and M-CORRECT only prepare candidates.
2. **Atomic record/index transition:** correction record and reverse-link entries are committed as one store transition. A partial state is non-conformant.
3. **No clean absence on incomplete coverage:** missing, stale, unreadable or divergent history yields UNKNOWN.
4. **No authority by access:** connector access, repository write permission, role label or self-asserted authority_ref does not establish authority.
5. **No authority leakage from ledger:** this gate's R2 admission authority does not grant G2 ledger mutation powers.
6. **No promotion of design evidence:** specified cases are not passed tests. A source inspection, fixture execution, independent reproduction and live observation must be labelled distinctly.
7. **No production mutation in acceptance fixtures:** all fixture stores, authority records, IDs and evidence are synthetic and isolated.
8. **No hidden migration:** the receipt gate does not migrate Phase 4 named evidence families, alter existing records, or create governed receipt identity before G1/G4.
9. **No retention overclaim:** policy obligations for backup/restore are not proof of operational backup implementation.
10. **No broad readiness inference:** every outcome is bounded to the exact scope, pins, revision and evaluation time.
