## The working rules (the anti-drift half — the point of this plan)

Rule 1 — SETTLED IS SETTLED. The authorities above are the
whole scope. A cleared artefact is not re-adjudicated, a ruled
question is not re-asked, and a preserved historical pin is not
re-litigated. Either seat may CITE settled material; neither
may re-open it.

Rule 2 — FINDINGS NAME THEIR CLAUSE. A finding that can hold a
checkpoint must cite the governing pin and the clause or chunk
line the work contradicts, or demonstrate a breach of an
existing rule of record. A proposal for something NO governing
pin contains is not a checkpoint finding: it is recorded,
shelved as a proposal, and routed to Ben — it does not hold the
block, and it is never silently absorbed into the scope.
(Ben's standing rule stands behind this: architecture changes
are his ruling, never an improvisation by either seat.)

Rule 3 — WHOLE ROUNDS, ONE CHECKPOINT PER BLOCK. Each block
ends in exactly one checkpoint bundle discharging its chunk
sheet line by line. Alex returns ONE adjudication covering the
whole bundle. If corrections are needed, they are drafted as
ONE chunked correction plan per checkpoint and built as one
round — never serial single-finding rounds. (Ben's process
rule, whole plan Part E, extended from Batch 4 to the whole
remaining path by this plan.)

Rule 4 — CHUNK SHEET BEFORE EVERY BUILD. Blocks 0 and 1 are
already sheeted. Blocks 2 and 3 get their chunk sheets drafted
from the spec pin and shelved BEFORE their builds begin; each
sheet rides for Alex's approval INSIDE the previous block's
checkpoint submission, so sheet approval never costs its own
round.

Rule 5 — PARALLEL LANES, ALWAYS. The letter that starts a
block's build simultaneously assigns Alex his lane work for
that block (crosswalk, adversarial matrix, inspection prep
against the sheet), so his review is warm when the bundle
lands. While Remmy builds, Alex works; while Alex reviews,
Remmy prepares the next block's sheet. Neither lane waits idle
on the other. (Ben's parallel-lanes rule, 2026-11-14.)

Rule 6 — PROBE BEFORE WORDING, WHERE A FACT IS DISPUTED. If a
block turns on a mechanism fact, the fact is established in the
room first and the paper is written from the transcript — the
order Ben authorised and Revs 6–8 proved. Probes are evidence,
shelved with the block's bundle.

Rule 7 — CADENCE. One letter at a time, acknowledgement before
the next; a fallback timer rides every letter; shelf-first —
the shelf is updated before Alex is sent to work and again on
his reply, before anyone replies further. Status reports touch
the live thing (process, log, pin), never momentum.

Rule 8 — BEN'S RESERVED MATTERS ARE UNTOUCHED. Spend, outreach,
publishes (including authorising shelf publication), accounts,
and the Block 4 pre-flight decisions remain Ben's. No subject
call occurs in Blocks 0–3. This plan authorises nothing new;
it disciplines what Ben already authorised.
