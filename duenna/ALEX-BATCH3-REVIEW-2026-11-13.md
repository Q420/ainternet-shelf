3. HIGH — Step-budget enforcement can contradict the record

In _run_step(), elapsed time is checked after _tool_step() or _model_step() has already emitted its events.

If a step exceeds its budget but its operation initially succeeds, _run_step() replaces the returned result with a failure. However, the earlier STEP_SATISFIED and passing CHECK_RESULT have already been written. The record can therefore say the step was satisfied while the driver's control flow treats it as failed.

There is a second test gap: step_advance_s advances the scripted clock after the elapsed-time check. It cannot demonstrate a tool-step timeout. The suite demonstrates tree and run budgets, but not both step budgets.

Required: move budget adjudication before the final satisfaction/failure events, preserve one coherent event outcome, and add independent over-budget tests for TOOL/RULE and MODEL steps.
