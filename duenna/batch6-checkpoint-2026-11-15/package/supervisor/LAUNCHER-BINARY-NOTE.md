The production launcher binary rides in this bundle as
launcher-production.b64 (base64 text, so the bundle stays
shelf-portable). Decode with `base64 -d`; the decoded bytes
sha256 to the C4-12 pin
9ea9b615a22bc7ea035d375fd3dd17c8fb5c7e8cdc9861a152882e382a637722,
verified live at C6-0b and again at bundle assembly. The
scratch-room launcher binaries in evidence/g2 and evidence/g3
are carried the same way (launcher-scratch.b64).
