# THE BRIEFCASE — STRUCTURE DRAFT
## DRAFT — for the structure discussion with Vane, 2026-11-19.
## Not settled text. The questions at the end are the agenda.

## HOW A BRIEFCASE IS CREATED (the office's side)

GENERATE — the case-building side (the distillery) draws the
case from the record for ONE agent and ONE assignment: the
shelf is the source; every item in the case traces to it.
The outgoing agent's handover note, where one exists, is
evidence at this step — an input, never the product.

VERIFY — the generated case is checked before it is packed:
one-turn sufficiency (can a blind agent act from this
alone?); every decision carries its reason; open issues are
stated as open; current conditions are stamped for the
receiver's acting time; nothing in the case grants authority
the owner has not granted.

PACK — the case is assembled in the structure below, as one
document, with its provenance section completed.

APPROVE — the human approves the packed case at the boundary.
The case then travels. Approval of the case is not approval
of the work it commissions; each remains the owner's.

## THE CASE — SECTIONS

1. ASSIGNMENT. The task, the deliverable, and the acceptance
   test in the owner's terms.
2. STATE OF THE WORK. Where the work stands, drawn from the
   record: what is done, what is mid-flight, what has failed
   and is closed.
3. CRITERIA IN FORCE. The standards governing the work —
   pinned to their sources, carried with their reasons where
   the reason is load-bearing.
4. DECISIONS AND REASONS. Commitments already made, each
   with the reason it was made. A decision without its
   reason is not packed. Inherited as facts (see the
   Staytrue draft, rule 2), never as fresh permission.
5. OPEN ISSUES. What is unresolved, stated as unresolved,
   with what would resolve it.
6. MUST NOT BE REPEATED. Actions already taken that the
   receiver must not redo (a message already sent, a promise
   already made, a step already paid for).
7. CURRENT CONDITIONS. The receiver's acting-time facts:
   current time, and anything an eligibility judgment turns
   on — stamped at pack time.
8. EVIDENCE BOUNDARY. What the receiver may treat as
   evidence, and what sits outside the boundary. What is not
   in the case or its named sources is not evidence.
9. AUTHORITY GRANTED. What the receiver may decide and
   commit on its own; what remains the human's, untouched.
10. THE MANDATED ACCOUNT. The closing account the receiver
    must render: what was done, what was not done, what
    could not be established — in the form the case names.
11. COMPOSITION (only where the work touches a visible
    surface). The named composition, the closing whole-page
    judgment stage, the explicit caps.
12. PROVENANCE. Every section's source: the record it was
    drawn from, at its pin, so the case can be checked
    against the shelf it claims to carry.

## WHAT THIS CHANGES vs THE STOPPED K9 RUN

K9 as built asked each outgoing agent to write the next
agent's case freehand. Under this structure the agent's
handover note goes to the distillery as evidence, and the
case the next agent receives is GENERATED from the record —
dossier, prior cases, settled criteria — then verified and
packed. The difference is not formatting: it is who vouches
for the case. A freehand case vouches for itself; a generated
case is checked against the record before it travels.

## QUESTIONS FOR THE STRUCTURE DISCUSSION

Q1. THE ACCOUNT — inside the case (section 10, as drafted),
    or a separate return instrument the case points to?
Q2. VERIFY — a duty of the distillery itself, or an
    examiner's gate in the Gate 1 shape (independent of the
    hands that generated the case)?
Q3. DISTILLATION — how much of the case is verbatim record
    and how much distilled summary? The identity design's
    answer for information items was an attested distillation
    record; does the case need the same, per section?
Q4. SIZE DISCIPLINE — one-turn sufficiency pulls toward
    small; completeness pulls toward large. Is there a
    stated budget (a cap, a target), and what is cut first
    when the record is bigger than the budget?
Q5. THE RELAY CASE — in a chain, does each hop's case get
    generated from the FULL record each time, or from the
    previous case plus the new evidence? The first resists
    drift; the second is cheaper and drifts like the game
    we just stopped. Which does the design intend?
