Letter 05 — Dictionary 0.5 amendments for owner review

Both rulings are incorporated as draft amendments. The owner’s distinction is preserved: REVIEW is a non-halting destination for second-look obligations; HARDENED is a grade above VERIFIED whose promotion rule remains undecided.

Nothing is frozen, approved, or implemented.

1. REVIEW — proposed dictionary entry

Category: Outcomes and states

REVIEW — The recorded holding state for an item requiring a second look, without itself halting the operation. An item in REVIEW remains subject to its second-look obligation until that obligation receives its recorded disposition.

FORM: The item is identified by reference and linked to an open review obligation. The obligation records the item, the reason it requires review, and its review status.

MEANING: The item genuinely requires a second look, and the recorded obligation is sufficient to identify what must be reviewed. REVIEW does not establish that the item is false, invalid, or in need of correction.

Boundary: REVIEW is a holding state, not a halt — ≠ STOP, which records a halt and binds the named operation until release. A flag (⚑) creates the second-look obligation; REVIEW is where the item is held under that obligation. Neither the flag nor REVIEW alone invalidates the item or changes its authority. REVIEW does not authorise the item to be interpreted or honoured where its defect prevents that.

2. MALFORMED — amended boundary

MALFORMED — A message fails VALID. It is not interpreted, repaired, or partially honoured.

FORM: The applicable VALID checks identify the conformance failure and the defective field or rule.

Boundary: A conformance failure — ≠ INCORRECT, which is a content failure; ≠ STOP, which is a separate halt act. A MALFORMED item is routed to REVIEW for a second look. MALFORMED alone does not initiate STOP or halt the operation. STOP applies only where the separate canonical-Shelf falsity condition is met.

This gives MALFORMED a positive destination without weakening its existing rule that the malformed message itself is not interpreted or partially honoured.

3. HARDENED — proposed dictionary entry

Category: Evidence and claims

HARDENED — A grade above VERIFIED. A claim or item is HARDENED only after it has first achieved VERIFIED and then satisfied an additional promotion condition. VERIFIED remains the base grade: an independent check reproduced the claim from its evidence by a named method. HARDENED records that something beyond that base verification has also been established.

FORM: A VERIFIED record exists and resolves. A further promotion record identifies the additional condition relied on, the evidence or records demonstrating that condition, and the authority or method under which promotion is recognised.

MEANING: The additional condition genuinely provides a stronger basis for confidence than the original verification. The promotion record must establish more than repetition of the original check.

Boundary: HARDENED implies VERIFIED, but VERIFIED does not imply HARDENED. HARDENED is a grade of evidential standing, not a claim that something is infallible, permanently true, or immune to later correction. It does not replace SUPPORTED, which concerns whether evidence establishes a claim, or CONTESTED, which records unresolved disagreement.

Promotion rule — candidates for owner ruling

The owner has not yet selected what the additional condition must be. These are the three candidate readings, kept distinct:

Candidate

	

Promotion condition

	

What it would establish




A — Independent re-verification

	

A second, independent route reproduces the claim against its source.

	

The claim survives a further check that does not merely repeat the first route.




B — Survival through contest or comparison

	

The claim survives a defined contest or comparison against competing evidence or positions.

	

The claim has withstood an adversarial or comparative challenge.




C — Proof under reliance

	

The claim is relied upon in an operation, and its correctness is established under that reliance.

	

The claim has held up when put to operational use.

These candidates are not equivalent. A is about independent checking; B is about surviving challenge; C is about demonstrated correctness under use. They could be alternatives, cumulative requirements, or a defined combination. No promotion rule is selected in this draft.

HARDENED promotion rule — reserved for owner ruling

The promotion condition is deliberately unresolved. Three candidates are presented:

Independent re-verification: a second independent route reproduces the claim against its source.

Survival through contest or comparison: the claim survives a defined contest or comparison against competing evidence or positions.
