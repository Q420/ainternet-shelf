# EXON — OWNER RULINGS 2026-11-16, ADDENDUM 02: YES

Ruled by Ben, 2026-11-16 15:06 MST, in his own words:

"So yes needs to go beside a category. A state category.
And yes will be the current state."

## The ruling

**YES is admitted as a word of the outcomes-and-states
category, with one meaning only: the current state.**

YES marks the state that is presently in force — of the
states a record names, YES is the one that currently holds.
It answers one question: *which state is live right now?*

YES carries no other load. It does not mean agreement with a
claim (that is an evidence grade: VERIFIED, HARDENED), it
does not authorise an act (that is a ruling), it does not
acknowledge a record (that is RECEIVED), and it is not a
gate's verdict (that is PASS). Those words keep their jobs
under the collision splits (EXON-DELEGATED-RULINGS-2026-11-16,
Ruling 3); YES is fenced to its one job the same way.

Relation to the settled vocabulary: a superseded state is a
former state; YES names its successor's standing — the state
that replaced it is the current one. Where a record lists
candidate states, YES sits beside the one in force.

## Effect

YES rides into the Dictionary at its next revision, alongside
DESTINATION (Addendum 01) and the collision splits. Dictionary
0.5's admitted text is unchanged by this filing. The ruling
supersedes Remmy's proposal of the same day (that "yes" not
be an EXON word at all): the owner found the one job the word
is fit for instead of retiring it.
