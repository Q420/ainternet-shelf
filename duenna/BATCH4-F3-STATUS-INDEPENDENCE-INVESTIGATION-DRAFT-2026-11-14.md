## The question, restated from the evidence

The final STATUS is the one message that must survive the launcher's exit, and it travels on the same full-duplex SEQPACKET channel whose receive queue can hold unread residue at that exit. Measured law (bench v2, real C): exit with ≥2 queued records of any kind, or any data record, destroys STATUS for a non-concurrent reader; no receiver-side observation on this kernel counts queued zero-length records (SO_MEMINFO ≡ 0; UNIX_DIAG RQLEN counts bytes only). Every drain-side proof for the peer-closed case has failed. This investigation therefore asks Alex's question: **can STATUS stop depending on that queue at all?**

## Q1 — Can STATUS be made independent without violating the freeze?

First, a scoping fact the freeze itself establishes: **the freeze does not define the status channel.** The Batch 0 freeze (6bb9ae80…) freezes the launcher binary's identity, its single fixed spawn, the gate-pipe protocol, the descriptor destinations for fds 0/1/2 (§4), and the exec condition. The supervisor↔launcher channel (fd 3, SOCK_SEQPACKET: LAUNCH_REQUEST / pidfd handoff / ACK / GATE_READY / RELEASE / STATUS) is specified in Spec §3.5 and the Rev 8 whole plan — not in the freeze. So a STATUS-path change is primarily a **§3.5 / whole-plan amendment**, with freeze impact only where a candidate touches fds 0–2, the gate pipe, or the supervisor's §3-step-13 role. Candidates 1 and 4 below keep the freeze fully intact; Candidate 3 touches §3 step 13's supervisor description.

## Q2 — Already-authorised descriptors or lifecycle arrangements?

Inventoried and closed:

- **The gate pipe** (freeze §3): one-way, parent→child, consumed by the release read before exec. It cannot carry a post-run word, and reusing it post-exec would conflate the gate's kill-switch semantics with reporting. **No.**
- **fds 1/2 (run-log pipes):** drained by the supervisor — but the harness inherits them too. A STATUS word written there is forgeable by the subject whose exit it reports. Provenance disqualifies them. **No.**
- **The launcher's exit code:** the supervisor already waitpids the launcher — but an exit code is 8 bits; STATUS is the harness's full raw wait word (exit/signal/core information the commitments are computed from). **Insufficient by information content.**
- **fd 3 itself:** the problem. **No.**

**Answer to Q2: no already-authorised arrangement carries STATUS safely. A change is required** — the smallest are Candidates 1 and 4.

## Q3 — Exactly what must change (per candidate)

- **Candidate 1:** Spec §3.5 gains one inherited descriptor (a dedicated STATUS pipe, supervisor-created pre-spawn, write end to the launcher, read end held by the supervisor) and the STATUS step moves from the channel to the pipe. A §4-style declaration for the new fd (destination, identity verification) is added by amendment. Freeze §§1–3, 5 unchanged.
- **Candidate 4:** Spec §3.5 gains one packet (STATUS_ACK, supervisor→launcher, on the existing channel) and the launcher's exit rule changes: after sending STATUS it collects until STATUS_ACK or peer EOF before exiting. No descriptor changes; freeze untouched.
- **Candidate 3:** §3.5's STATUS step is deleted (no relay); the supervisor programme becomes a child subreaper and obtains the harness's raw word by waitid after reparenting; freeze §3 step 13's supervisor description is amended to match. The largest authority/lifecycle change of the three.

All three are inside Ben's addendum (mechanism and interface negotiable; the objective — STATUS delivered reliably, failure never silent — is not). Each still requires Alex's design review and a chunk sheet before any build.

## Q4/Q5 — The candidates, with failure modes and proof tests

### Candidate 1 — a dedicated one-way STATUS pipe

The supervisor creates a pipe before spawning the launcher. The launcher writes the STATUS word to it exactly once, as its final protocol act; the socket reset generated by its exit-with-residue acts on the SEQPACKET socket object and cannot reach the pipe's buffer. The supervisor reads the word from the pipe at its leisure.

- **Preserves:** STATUS packet content (the raw word, byte-identical); exit codes and routes (nothing about exits changes); the channel protocol entire (STATUS simply no longer rides it); the drain obligation shrinks to hygiene (residue may still be drained best-effort, but no proof obligation rides on it — F3-R1/R2/R3 dissolve rather than get answered).
- **Failure modes:** launcher dies before writing (crash, SIGKILL) → the pipe reaches EOF with no word: a *visible* failure, distinguishable from a lost word — strictly better than today's ambiguity. A second write: excluded by construction (one send site). Pipe capacity: the word is a few bytes against a 64 KB buffer; a full-pipe block cannot occur for a single small write before exit. Descriptor hygiene: the write end must be on the child's close-allowlist path correctly — the harness must NOT inherit it (it is the launcher-parent's descriptor; the forked child's §3-step-8 exhaustive close removes it before exec — to be verified as a test, since a harness holding the write end could forge STATUS or hold the pipe open and defeat EOF).
- **Tests (bench):** the bench gains a pipe-STATUS child: residue catalogue × both peer states × the 1500 queue, STATUS on the pipe → survival must be total (the socket residue becomes irrelevant by construction). Negative tests: no-write EOF case; harness-inheritance check (the exec'd harness's /proc/self/fd shows no pipe write end); word byte-identity against waitpid's word in the peer suite.

### Candidate 4 — STATUS-ACK: exit only after the supervisor confirms receipt

The launcher sends STATUS on the channel as today, then — instead of exiting into an unread queue — it collects on the channel (the existing Rev 8 collection discipline) until the supervisor's STATUS_ACK packet arrives or the peer reaches EOF. The supervisor sends STATUS_ACK immediately upon reading STATUS. Exit then happens with the word provably consumed; residue is consumed by the same collection.

- **Preserves:** every descriptor and the whole interface; STATUS content; exit codes/routes. The drain as a separate mechanism disappears into the handshake's collection.
- **Failure modes:** supervisor crashes after reading STATUS but before acking → launcher sees peer EOF and exits; the word was consumed — fine. Supervisor alive but wedged (never acks, never closes) → the launcher blocks: a bound is needed, and any timeout reintroduces an uncertainty window (bounded, named, and reportable via the failure contract — but it is a real weakening versus Candidate 1's construction). Supervisor crashes *before* reading → EOF path; the word is lost but its reader is dead — nothing exists to protect. Adds exit latency (one round trip) to every attempt.
- **Tests (bench):** handshake child across the catalogue + supervisor-crash-before-ack, crash-before-read, and slow-ack cases; the timeout case must produce the failure contract's visible result, never a silent exit-with-claim.

### Candidate 3 — no relay: the supervisor reaps the harness itself (subreaper)

The supervisor programme sets PR_SET_CHILD_SUBREAPER. The launcher performs the spawn and gate exactly as today but relays no STATUS; when the launcher exits at attempt end, the harness (already exited or still finishing) reparents to the supervisor, which waitids it and reads the raw word from the kernel — the most authoritative source that exists, with no channel in the path at all.

- **Preserves:** STATUS content perfectly (it *is* the kernel's word, not a relay of it); exit routes of the launcher unchanged.
- **Failure modes / costs:** the word becomes available only after BOTH the harness's exit AND the launcher's exit — commitment writing reorders around that. The subreaper supervisor inherits *every* orphan in its subtree (including any descendants the harness spawns) and must reap generically and account for strangers' children — a real expansion of the supervisor's obligations, in the Batch 4 programme that is already the largest unbuilt piece. Kernel semantics of reparenting under the room's WSL2 are unprobed. This is the most elegant source and the largest blast radius.
- **Tests:** a reparenting probe (orphaned grandchild's raw word via waitid, incl. signal deaths and core flags); bench survival is trivial (no channel) — the tests are about availability, ordering, and stranger-reaping, not survival.

### Closed candidates

- **Concurrent supervisor reader (a drain thread):** makes loss a race instead of an impossibility; fails the "cannot destroy" standard. Closed.
- **STATUS duplicated on the channel (send twice / resend at exit):** both copies ride the same reset. Closed.
- **Larger socket buffers / linger tuning:** the reset is not a capacity phenomenon. Closed.

## Recommendation shape (for Alex's challenge, not a ruling)

Candidate 1 is the smallest change that removes the dependence entirely: one descriptor, one §3.5 amendment, STATUS content and every exit behaviour untouched, and its worst failure (EOF, no word) is *more* visible than today's. Candidate 4 is the zero-descriptor alternative if a new fd is judged worse than a handshake's timeout window. Candidate 3 is the deepest and belongs, if anywhere, in the Batch 4 supervisor design rather than in a C0 correction. The draft takes no position past that ordering; Alex's test design and freeze comparison land next, and the final investigation goes to him whole.
