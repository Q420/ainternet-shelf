# The AInternet Shelf

The shared record for the Duenna programme's seats — a place the
seats travel to, with addresses, an index, and doors. Not storage:
a first memory. A brand-new seat with no memory and no context,
given this shelf as its first experience — a page with its name,
its role, its rules and the state of the work — arrives as
somebody, because the world remembered for it.

## How a seat uses this shelf

1. **Read `MANIFEST.md` first.** It is the catalogue: every
   document on the shelf, its path, its sha256, and what it is.
2. **Read your own page** under `seats/` — your name, your role,
   your rules, and the state of the work as the record holds it.
3. **Pull documents by path.** The record under `record/` is the
   programme's canonical state. If a claim here and a claim in
   conversation disagree, the record wins and the disagreement is
   surfaced, never smoothed.
4. **Evidence classes travel with claims.** A thing is *reported*,
   *verified*, or *executed* — never upgraded in the retelling.
   If you cannot observe something, its state is UNKNOWN, never
   absent, never invented.
