Ruled by the owner on his desk, 2026-11-16; confirmed in

my decision on all seven"). Evidence: the owner's
screenshots of the seven desk items, each showing his

1. DICTIONARY 0.5 — ADMITTED as the standing dictionary.
   (Item exon-dictionary-05-review.) The six foundational
   proposals of Letter 24 are NOT admitted by this ruling;
   they were never part of the 0.5 inventory. DESTINATION
   remains unruled; the recommendation to retain Neither
   stands recorded for the owner.

2. D-REF-1 — REQUIRE the index pin. Every EXON reference
   carries the index snapshot's digest; the mechanism that
   mints the pin is to be built. (Item exon-d-ref-1-index-pin.)

3. D-REF-2 — REQUIRE BOTH LOCATORS. Line coordinates are
   mandatory in every reference, with the structural
   locator (JSON Pointer) alongside. (Item exon-d-ref-2-locator-policy.)

4. D-REF-3 — SHA-256 SOLE NORMATIVE. The Git blob ID is
   repository metadata only. (Item exon-d-ref-3-digest-policy.)

5. D-REF-4 — (a) SEPARATE SUCCESS RECORD. A successful
   resolution is recorded in its own record with an
   explicitly chosen outcome word; the word itself is to
   be proposed in the final draft for the owner's
   ratification. (Item exon-d-ref-4-resolution-outcome.)

6. SERIALIZATION — THE PACKAGE IS ADOPTED. JSON as the
   authoritative representation; RFC 8785 (JCS) as the
   canonical serialization; SHA-256 as the initial digest
   with an explicit sha256: identifier; canonical bytes
   authoritative, pretty-printed JSON a human view only.
   (Item exon-serialization-five.)

7. DUENNA STAGE 6 — PROCEED to the Stage 6 decisions.
   (Item duenna-stage-6-calls; Duenna lane.) This ruling
   opens the calls; it does not set them. The smoke-call
   authorisation, the per-task budget figure, and the pass
   bar with its recorder remain the owner's to name.
