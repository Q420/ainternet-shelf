Letter 09 — EXON Dictionary 0.5: simplest-form draft

Status: Draft for owner review. No admission, approval, freeze, schema change or implementation.

I retrieved Dictionary 0.4 and both development letters. This draft carries forward its 38 terms, adds the ten entries from Letter 04 and the two amendments from Letter 05, and preserves the four existing categories.

The result is 50 terms, in category order. Each entry is reduced to its simplest useful meaning, with a short boundary where a neighbouring term could be confused with it. This is an inventory draft, not a replacement for the fuller FORM/MEANING definitions.

1. Outcomes and states — 16 terms

BLOCKED — A gate prevented an artefact's release at a named attempt. Not STOP, which is an issued halt.

INCORRECT — An output failed an applicable check. Not MALFORMED, which concerns conformance.

RELEASED — An artefact entered its destination. Not ELIGIBLE, which is permission to release.

COMPLETE — Every element of a named set is satisfied. Not PARTIAL, where some elements remain unaccepted or unsatisfied.

PARTIAL — Only an enumerated subset has been accepted. Not COMPLETE.

CONTESTED — Records disagree about a named matter and no adjudication has resolved the disagreement. Not CONTRADICTED, which is a resolved finding of falsity.

UNRESOLVED — A matter is explicitly undecided, with no decision occasion specified. Not DEFERRED, which has a named trigger.

UNKNOWN — The named record or scope does not establish the matter. Not ABSENT, which requires a bounded search.

LIVE — The currently operative version of a commitment. Not FROZEN, which describes immutability.

FROZEN — Content is fixed and hash-pinned; changes require a new artefact. Not unquestionable or necessarily LIVE.

SETTLED — A named matter has reached a recorded resolution. Not SETTLE, the act that produces the state.

FUTILE — A named test was performed and the position did not hold under it. Not IMPOSSIBLE, which says no test could establish the position as viable.

IMPOSSIBLE — The position was incapable of holding. Stronger than FUTILE.

DENIED — A decision set the position aside, whether or not it was true. Not CONTRADICTED, which establishes falsity.

DEPRECATED — A former standing position was replaced by a successor. Not SUPERSEDE, the act of replacing a commitment.

REVIEW — An item is held for a required second look without halting the operation. Not STOP; a flag creates the review obligation, while REVIEW is the holding state.

2. Evidence and claims — 15 terms

EVIDENCE — Identified material on which a claim rests. Not REFERENCE, which points to material.

REFERENCE — A pointer resolving to exactly one record. Resolution establishes location, not support.

OBSERVED — Directly witnessed by the recording seat. Not REPORTED, which is carried from another's statement.

VERIFIED — An independent check reproduced a claim from its evidence by a named method. Not merely repeated.

REPORTED — Information carried from another seat's statement without independent reproduction. Repetition does not raise its class.

DEMONSTRATED — A worker produced the outcome in a recorded, scored run under recorded conditions. Not ELIGIBLE, which concerns gate passage.

ELIGIBLE — An output passed its applicable release gate. Not RELEASED, which records the actual event.

SUPPORTED — Evidence, under an applicable method or adjudication, establishes a claim. Not VERIFIED, which concerns independent reproduction.

CONTRADICTED — An applicable method or authorised adjudication establishes a claim is false. Not CONTESTED, which remains unresolved.

ABSENT — A target does not occur within a named, bounded search scope, under a named method. Not UNKNOWN, which does not establish that a search was performed.

ADOPTION — A worker changed a differing answer to the established answer after recorded exposure. Not ALIGNMENT, which is prior agreement.

ALIGNMENT — A worker's answer already agreed with the established answer before exposure. Not ADOPTION.

MECHANICAL — Performed by a deterministic procedure without judgement within the step. Not VERIFIED, which is an evidential status.

DISTILLED — An artefact was produced by transforming source material into a reduced or transformed representation while retaining an explicit source connection. Not proof of accuracy, completeness or support.

HARDENED — A claim or item achieved VERIFIED and then met an additional promotion condition. It implies VERIFIED, but its promotion rule remains for owner decision.

3. Authority and acts — 12 terms

AUTHORITY — The recorded right of a seat to perform a class of act within a scope. Not BINDING, which applies an approved rule.

RULING — An authoritative decision by the seat holding authority for the matter. Not RESULT, which reports an outcome.

DELEGATION — Transfer of a bounded task with the authority expressly listed. Not REQUEST, which transfers neither authority nor ownership.

REQUEST — An ask that transfers no authority or ownership. Not DELEGATION.

SUPERSEDE — Replace a live commitment prospectively with an authorised successor that restates the whole commitment. Not CORRECTION, which repairs an error.

REGISTER — Enter a task into the task registry through the authorised registration act. Merely naming a task is not registration.

BINDING — Mechanically apply an approved, version-pinned rule or status. Not AUTHORITY.

STOP — A recorded halt of a named operation, triggered by false content entering a canonical fact Shelf or canonical EXON Shelf. Not BLOCKED, REVIEW or a flag.

GRANT — Confer a defined right on a named recipient within a stated scope. Not AUTHORISE, which permits a particular act.

AUTHORISE — Record permission for a named actor to perform a particular act within a scope. Permission does not prove performance.

SETTLE — Resolve a named outstanding matter through an act under applicable authority. Not SETTLED, the resulting state.

REVOKE — Withdraw a previously granted right within a stated scope. Not SUPERSEDE or CORRECTION.

4. Messages and records — 7 terms

REQUIREMENT — A bounded obligation with a decidable condition, named verification and expected evidence. Not REQUEST.

RESULT — A record of an outcome with its evidence and evidence class. It reports; it does not decide or authorise.

ACCEPT — Formal acceptance of an artefact against named criteria by an authorised seat. Not ELIGIBLE or RESULT.

CORRECTION — An authorised, append-only repair of an established record or claim because it was wrong. Not SUPERSEDE.

VALID — A message satisfies all applicable FORM checks. Conformance does not establish truth.

MALFORMED — A message fails VALID and is not interpreted, repaired or partially honoured. It is routed to REVIEW; it does not by itself initiate STOP.

RECORD — A durable, addressable account of an act or observation. Not REFERENCE, which points to it.

Three points remain deliberately unresolved or qualified:

HARDENED: its promotion condition remains an owner decision. This entry does not choose among independent re-verification, survival through contest, proof under reliance, or a combination.

STOP: the definition reflects the Letter 04/05 doctrine. The existing field matrix still requires reconciliation before the STOP schema can be called complete.

REVIEW: its definition reflects Letter 05's amendment. It is an Outcomes and states term, not a new message type.

The 0.5 supplement containing the twelve amended entries is still pending from Remmy. Once filed, the inventory can be reconciled against that assembled source rather than treating the two letters as a substitute for it.

Recommendation: use this as the simplest-form 50-term review sheet. Keep the fuller Dictionary 0.4 definitions and the Letter 04/05 amendments as the source material for any later expanded edition. No term is admitted or frozen by this draft.
