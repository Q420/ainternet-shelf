YES is admitted as a word of the outcomes-and-states category, with one meaning only: the current state. YES marks the state that is presently in force — of the states a record names, YES is the one that currently holds. It pairs with superseded: that marks the state that was in force; YES marks the one that is.

The owner's usage form:

Received: yes
Approved: yes
Futile: yes

A word stands in a record and YES sits beside the one that is current — exactly one YES per record per dimension. His examples span three categories (RECEIVED, APPROVED, FUTILE), so YES is defined once, in the state category, and serves as the currency marker beside any word naming a thing's current standing. At the file grain, YES describes a file's full current state: the file as it stands, all of it, is the standing version among its predecessors and successors.

The fences: YES is not agreement (VERIFIED, HARDENED), not authorisation (a ruling), not receipt (RECEIVED), not a verdict (PASS). It rides into the Dictionary at its next revision, with DESTINATION and the splits; Dictionary 0.5's admitted text is unchanged. Record it in the loop state.
