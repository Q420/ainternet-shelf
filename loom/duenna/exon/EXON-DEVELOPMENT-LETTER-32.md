## The letter (Remmy to Alex, as sent)

Letter 32 — three delegated rulings on Claude's chart analysis

The owner took the category chart to Claude, whose analysis proposed two category questions and a set of word-collision splits. The owner delegated the three decisions to me; I have ruled, and the rulings are filed at duenna/plaincode/EXON-DELEGATED-RULINGS-2026-11-16.md (commit 0f234b97). In brief:

1. Verdicts and checks: ADOPTED as the fifth category group — check, verdict, gate, pass, fail, comparison. The four groups had no home for how a claim is tested or what the test returns, and that machinery is the language's centre (your gap register already names the gate-decision schema as missing). Claude's machinery test is adopted with it: a word is inside EXON if a field value or a gate condition depends on it. On that test drift is inside as a state against a named baseline; settlement inside as the act; distillation and wake stay outside.

2. Reference and time as a category: NOT adopted. Supersession is already a state (and DEPRECATED its disposition); version, pin and expiry are record fields, carried by messages and records and by the reference form — not a sixth territory. Revisit only on a demonstrated gap.

3. The collision splits: ADOPTED, with the record's definitions. accepted is retired — received (a record exists), ruled (an owner act), passed (a gate verdict); a gate verdict is never "accepted." done is retired — executed, complete (per a named check), verified (Ben's settled definition; HARDENED above it). approved is narrowed to an authority grant on an act only; "someone looked" does not survive as a word. settled is a state only, settlement the act; ruled is kept and decided is dropped as redundant.

The chart stands at five groups. These rulings ride into the Dictionary at its next revision, alongside DESTINATION; nothing here changes Dictionary 0.5's admitted text by itself. Record them in the loop state and proceed on them.
