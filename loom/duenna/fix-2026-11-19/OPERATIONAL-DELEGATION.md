## What is granted

The adjudicator may settle the adjudicated outcome of a
production run against that run's pinned contract, and
produce the adjudication record the governance record
requires — for a run whose adjudication package has first
passed Gate 1 verification, recorded with the verifier's
identity and result preserved. The settlement may be
INDETERMINATE where the evidence does not support a
stronger conclusion.

The other roles in the chain — the examiner at Gate 1, the
orchestrator assembling — are as the governance record
states. This instrument grants nothing to them and
restates nothing of theirs: one fact, one home.

## Scope

Production runs of the Duenna programme, each under its
own pinned run contract. One delegation, applied per run:
for each adjudication, this instrument must be shown to
cover that run and that decision, or the package fails
Gate 1.

## Limits

- No scope approval, no work started, no programme change
  authorised. This grant is settlement authority only;
  authority to act is not implied.
- No decision on an unverified package. A decision taken
  without a recorded Gate 1 verification is not a
  settlement under this delegation.
- No alteration of the gate structure, the evidence set,
  or this delegation's own terms. Changes return to Ben.
- UNKNOWN stays UNKNOWN and disagreement stays recorded,
  inside the settlement itself.
- This delegation is not transferable, and is not inferred
  by any other seat from identity or possession of
  materials.

## Operative condition and end

This delegation is operative from Ben's issuance until he
revokes or replaces it; revocation and replacement are
recorded on the shelf in the same manner as issuance. An
adjudication has begun, for this clause, when its package
has been assembled and recorded at Status 1 (PACKAGE
ASSEMBLED) naming this delegation. An adjudication begun
while the delegation was operative stands; an adjudication
not yet begun at revocation may not begin under it.
