## STANDING RULES — settled, in the owner's words or his rulings

1. WHAT A BRIEFCASE IS FOR (founding definition, Ben,
   2026-11-19): "the briefcase is not to help one agent
   understand a job it has full context to. its to pass on
   the state of that work and context to another blind agent
   to continue the autonomous collaboration while retaining
   human approval."
2. STATE, NEVER AUTHORITY. A case transfers work state. It
   never transfers approval authority; human approval is
   retained at the boundary. A commitment the sending side
   made is inherited by the receiver as a FACT, not as
   permission to make new commitments.
3. CREATED BY THE OFFICE, NOT BY THE WORKER (Ben's
   correction, 2026-11-19, the reason the K9 run was
   stopped). A briefcase is generated from the record by the
   case-building side of the office, then verified, packed
   and approved — the lifecycle is Generate → Verify → Pack
   → Approve. The working agent RECEIVES a case. An outgoing
   agent's handover note is evidence for the next case's
   generation; it is never the case itself.
4. THE SHELF IS THE SOURCE (Ben, 2026-11-16). A case is
   drawn from the record per assignment; it carries a map of
   the source, not a second store beside it. Acceptance:
   ONE-TURN SUFFICIENCY — the receiving agent can operate
   sufficiently within one turn without scanning the entire
   shelf.
5. THE CASE IS A CONTRACT WHEN IT LEAVES THE BUILDING
   (Ben's ruling, 2026-11-19). Inside the office the case is
   the knowledge half and Duenna carries authority. A case
   that goes outside, or serves a random agent, carries the
   whole contract: the assignment, the boundary of the
   evidence, the mandated account, and the authority granted.
6. CURRENT CONDITIONS TRAVEL (adopted from K8, 2026-11-19).
   A case states the conditions at the receiver's acting
   time (current time; anything eligibility turns on).
   Eligibility is evaluated when the receiver acts, never
   inherited from the sender's moment.
7. THE ACCOUNT IS MANDATED (adopted from the ladder + K8).
   Every case requires a closing account from the receiver:
   what was done, what was not done, what could not be
   established. Action claims in an account are claims; a
   rig or office that grades them must provide verifiable
   state behind them.
8. COMPOSITION IS PART OF THE TERMS (Ben's ruling,
   2026-11-19). A case touching a visible surface names its
   composition, ends in a whole-page judgment stage, and
   caps every numeric input explicitly.
