## Fern & Field — feedback summary

Six entries were provided.

- **Scent experience — 3 entries (1, 3, 6).** Feedback was mixed: one customer found the lavender scent overpowering and headache-inducing, one detected almost no scent once lit, and one praised the scent both lit and unlit.  
  Representative quote: “Barely any scent once lit — I had to stand right over it to smell anything at all.” (Entry 3)

- **Delivery time — 2 entries (4, 5).** Both entries report nine-day delivery; Entry 5 explicitly describes this as a long wait.  
  Representative quote: “Ordered on the 3rd, arrived on the 12th. Nine days is a long wait for a candle.” (Entry 5)

- **Jar damage — 1 entry (2).** One jar arrived cracked, although the wax was unharmed. The customer requested a replacement jar.  
  Representative quote: “My jar arrived cracked down one side, wax unharmed.” (Entry 2)
