## What is granted

The adjudicator may settle the adjudicated outcome of a
production run against that run's pinned contract, and
produce the adjudication record the governance record
requires — for a run whose adjudication package has
first passed Gate 1 verification, recorded with the
verifier's identity and result preserved. The settlement
may be INDETERMINATE where the evidence does not support
a stronger conclusion.

The other roles in the chain — the examiner at Gate 1,
the orchestrator assembling — are as the governance
record states. This instrument grants nothing to them
and restates nothing of theirs: one fact, one home.

## Scope
