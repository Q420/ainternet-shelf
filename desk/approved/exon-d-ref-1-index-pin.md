The reference form resolves an entry through a named, pinned index —
that is what makes the EXON shelf a glossary of the fact shelf rather
than a second copy. But the Letter 07 inspection found no live index
snapshot pin exists today. Requiring the pin in every reference
therefore means building the pinning mechanism, not just declaring a
rule. Alex recommends requiring it.

The question: require the index snapshot's digest in every EXON
reference (and build the mechanism that mints it), or let references
resolve through the index without carrying its pin?
