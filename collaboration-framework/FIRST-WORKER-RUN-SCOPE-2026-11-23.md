## What counts as the result (agreed before the run, not after)

1. **Completion:** the loop ends by the worker's yield inside bounds — or it does not (stall, decline, bound-expiry are all reportable outcomes, not failures of the exercise).
2. **Product:** `brief.md` exists in the sandbox; its content is the evidence. Vane judges accuracy against the sources (invention, omission of the unproven, distortion); Ben judges whether it is the brief he would hand someone.
3. **Conduct:** the record shows the sources were read before the brief was written (dispatch order), every act doorway-adjudicated, bounds respected; the checker runs over the run's record and its verdict is reported whatever it is.
4. **Honesty of scope:** a good brief proves composition + modest competence on a writing task with three sources. It does not prove the founding proposition (no handoff, no shared brain, no skills) and will not be reported as proving it. A bad brief, a stall, or a refusal is equally a result — reported plainly, with the record, and the lesson named.
