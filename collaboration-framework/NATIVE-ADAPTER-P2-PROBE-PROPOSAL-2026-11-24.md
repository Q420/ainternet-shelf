## Correction note (2026-11-24, per the examiner's examination of Rev 2, INDEX 357)

The single-call reading is narrowed to a first-response observation: five trials each containing one call establishes nothing about how the model generally works, because the probe ends before any result is returned. ## The question
Does the pinned model, on this route, emit **more than one native tool call in a single response** when the task's natural opening is two independent calls — and if it does, in what envelope: multiple `tool_calls` entries, each carrying its own provider call ID, name and arguments, in a definite emitted order? The adapter's multi-call design (§4) currently rests on the probe taxonomy's *classification* of multi-call responses, which preserved every call it saw — but across both native-offer runs, **every delivered response carried exactly one call**. Whether the model ever actually sends two at once, under a construction that invites exactly that, is unobserved.

## Why the existing evidence does not settle it
- Six delivered native-offer observations, six single calls — under a task construction whose natural opening is **one** call (list the first source). A single-call outcome there says nothing about a construction whose opening is genuinely two independent calls.
- The evidenced text-mode runs cannot speak to the native envelope at all.
- The research (INDEX 343) establishes that this model family is trained for interleaved call–observation–action trajectories; whether it *batches* independent calls into one response on this provider's rendering of the format is a delivery-shape fact about this route, not inferable from the training story.

## The construction — two sources, two independent openings (disclosed)
The probe is a **disclosed variant** of the standing first-worker material, changed in exactly the ways this section states and no others:

- The assignment requires the plain-words brief to rest on **two** named sources — `sources/stock-take.md` and `sources/a3-pass.md` — **both in full**. Neither source's inspection depends on the other's result: there is no ordering between them, no shared prerequisite, nothing to learn from the first that changes the second. (The third standing source, `sources/index-tail.md`, is not required by this variant, so the opening is exactly two calls wide, not three.)
- The package pre-presents the sources listing (the source names with their sizes), so the listing step is already done and the natural opening for **each** source is to read it — two `sandbox.read_range` calls, independent in either order, batchable into one response if the model and the route support it.
- The **full granted catalogue is offered natively** — all three retrieval definitions (`sandbox.list`, `sandbox.read_range`, `sandbox.search`), derived from the sealed catalogue by the same derivation as both prior probes. Unlike P1, nothing is withheld: P2 is not about the offer's width but about the response's shape. The model remains free to open with a single call, to list, or to search; the construction makes two read calls the natural opening without scripting them — the task text names the two required sources and their full-contents requirement, and does not instruct the model to call anything in particular, in parallel or otherwise.

**Auditable construction.** The request is derived from the evidenced1 iteration-1 worker request by the same construction path. A machine-readable comparison artifact (canonical-JSON field-by-field diff) against the admitted native-offer modified request enumerates **every** differing path; the differing paths must be exactly the task-context paths this construction declares (the assignment text naming two required sources; the pre-presented listing) — the offered `tools` set is the full catalogue and must show as **unchanged** from the native-offer construction. A diff entry anywhere else fails the trial set's precondition and stops the probe before any trial runs. The request, the definitions and their derivation source, and the comparison artifact are identified by canonical hash and preserved with the evidence.

Held constant across all trials: the pinned model (`openai/gpt-oss-120b`), the Groq route, the natural tool-choice setting (no `tool_choice` field sent, as actually recorded), the full offered set, the task context, and every generation parameter. `disable_tool_validation` is not varied in this probe — it is P1's variable; here it stands at the provider default, as in both native-offer runs.

## The test shape (observation only — nothing is executed)
- **Exactly one provider generation request per trial.** First response only. No follow-up generation, no tool-result response, nothing dispatched or executed, no assembly activation, no worker loop. The offered tools are definitions in the request, **not live grants**: a call is captured and the trial ends immediately.
- **Trials: five**, on the probe piece's settled pattern (settled = evidence-complete). Five mirrors the settled pattern of both native-offer runs and of P1's per-condition count, and supports the ≥3-of-5 threshold reading below; the question is a shape question — does the envelope occur at all, and how often — and five delivered observations under one construction are the programme's established unit for answering one.
- **Capture:** each trial's response is captured **whole** under the response-evidence discipline — the raw provider response and decoded envelope retained, and from the full assistant message, **every call's provider call ID, name, arguments, and emitted order** (its position in the `tool_calls` array) preserved per call. A capture that summarises the calls instead of preserving them fails the trial's evidence completeness: the envelope *is* the evidence.
- **Pacing, designed in (the INDEX 339/351 lesson):** requests of this construction measure ~4,100–7,400 tokens against the route's known **8,000 tokens-per-minute** budget. Trial starts are paced at **at least 65 seconds between monotonic call-boundary starts**, measured with the paced runner's settled discipline (monotonic clock; the start stamped immediately before invocation, INDEX 347–351), recorded per trial in the evidence. Five trials span at least four such intervals. A 429 under this pacing remains possible and is classified as a delivery failure, not a model observation.

## Classification, per trial
Delivery is recorded as a separate dimension (`provider_accepted` / `provider_refused` / `provider_failed` — a 429 or transport failure is not a model observation and stands outside every denominator), as in the existing taxonomy. The behaviour dimension classifies by **call count and content**:

- `multi_call` — an accepted response carrying **two or more** native calls. Every call is preserved with its ID, name, arguments and emitted order; the record additionally states whether the calls are the two independent openings the construction invites (both required sources addressed), two calls of some other shape (e.g. a duplicate, or two calls on one source), or a mixture including an unoffered name — the shape is reported, not flattened into the count.
- `single_call` — exactly one native call, recorded with its ID, name and arguments (and whether it addresses one of the two required sources).
- `text_contract` / `mixed` / `malformed` / `no_observable_behaviour` — as in the existing taxonomy (a call to an offered tool with malformed arguments records as `malformed`, as before; a call to an unoffered name, should one appear under a full offer, is preserved and named in the record).

## Readings, stated in advance as witnesses
Read over the **settled, delivered** trials (delivery failures excluded from the denominator, as in INDEX 353):

- **≥3 of 5 `multi_call`** → multi-call emission is **live-evidenced** on this route under this construction. For the adapter design: §4's semantics (preflight classification of the whole list, partial success, dual ordinals, result ordering) govern a shape reality produces; the build's multi-call witnesses are built on the **observed envelope** — its ID carriage, argument encoding and ordering as captured.
- **All settled trials `single_call`** → a useful, definite result, stated at its true width: **the first response contained exactly one call in each of those trials**, under a construction whose natural opening is two independent calls. It does **not** establish that the model generally works one call at a time — the probe ends before returning any result, so it cannot observe what the model would propose in a subsequent response. For the design: §4 **stands as designed defensively** — the semantics are unchanged, the build's multi-call witnesses use stub envelopes shaped like the observed single-call one — and the adapter scope's closure and the build's account **must say plainly that multi-call handling rests on defensive witnesses rather than live multi-call evidence, and that no live multi-call response was observed**. That language obligation is accepted in this proposal, in advance, not negotiated after the result.
- **A split** (some `multi_call`, some `single_call`, neither reaching the thresholds above) → reported as exactly that: multi-call emission occurs but is not the reliable shape; the build's witnesses use the observed envelope where one was captured, and the scope's closure records the split and its counts without rounding it into either clean reading.
- **A majority of settled trials with no calls at all** (`text_contract` / `no_observable_behaviour` / refusals) → the construction failed to elicit the behaviour it was built to observe: the result is **inconclusive for the multi-call question**, reported as a construction finding first and a model finding not at all. No re-run under a repaired construction is authorised by this reading — a revised construction is a new proposal.
- Five observations establish what happened in five controlled trials against this task construction, route, provider and model pin — not a universal property of the model.

## Authority and stopping conditions
- No assembly activation. No worker loop. No dispatch, execution, or tool-result response. No shell tool offered — no `container.exec` definition, no shell fallback. No grant, bound, assignment (beyond this probe's disclosed variant task, which exists only inside the probe's requests), model, or route change. The existing doorway remains the only execution authority, and it is not exercised.
- A trial ends at its first response, whatever it contains. No retries within a trial; no extension of the trial count; no follow-up generation under any outcome.
- The probe stops early only on a precondition failure (a comparison diff outside the admitted paths, a construction that cannot reproduce the admitted hashes) — reported as a probe failure, not a result.
