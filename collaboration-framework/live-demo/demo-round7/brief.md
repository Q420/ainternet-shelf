I have prepared a brief on the collaborative agent framework. The framework is a system of persistent seats that interact through a bounded record‑and‑doorway mechanism, with an accountability foundation that has been implemented and tested. Three implementation pieces – A1, A2 and A3 – have been passed: they define how a worker instance is assembled, how grants are enforced, and how a bounded loop perceives, requests, acts and accounts for outcomes. The adapter has been built and its transport and deadline behaviour examined, but its evidence chain remains open until a live‑record replay is performed.
