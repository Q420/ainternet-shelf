# Vane's scoping replies — Piece 6 orchestrator functions (§3.8) (2026-11-22)

**Via Pulse Bridge, PB pb-mwsoa012-22-fg8r. Scope examined @ f5a3ec99. Decision: SCOPE CORRECTION REQUIRED BEFORE BUILD — architecture approved in direction.**

The diagnosis is sound — real gaps, not cosmetic additions. Decisions and refinements:

- **S1 approved in direction:** reference-by-position for the commission's tree and gate declaration, full crew list in ASSEMBLY_PROPOSED; the proposal identifies the exact commission event it answers, and the fold/doorway establishes the referenced terms are the terms actually commissioned. Added requirement: a re-proposal must be distinguishable from the previous one — an assembly `correct` followed by a new proposal must not leave the old assembly record eligible to support it.
- **S2 approved:** the separate examiner act is essential — the orchestrator proposes, the examiner records the assessment, neither impersonates the other, neither is an outcome. The record identifies the exact proposal it examines; its criteria mapping covers every proposed tree part and the relevant commission criteria; its approved gate set is the set actually examined, not merely a copy of the proposal; its identity assessment covers every proposed seat with the status weighed. A new proposal requires a new record. The doorway enforces completeness and consistency; substantive correctness remains the examiner's.
- **S3 — coverage semantics need precision:** deriving coverage and enforcing statuses is agreed, but what determines which contributions a certification covers must be answered explicitly (whole run always? or a represented, validated selection?). Once settled: every contributing seat within the declared coverage appears with its derived status — no omission, misstatement, or invented status; no onboarding record = `unverified`.
- **S4 approved in direction:** COMMENT + `to_seat` + the deferred wake; no new act type for a note. Boundaries: the note still obeys R10 (attached to a contribution or the job — no free-floating channel); routing must identify an actual recorded disagreement and a valid gate joint, with the validation and derived representation specified, so `routes_disagreement` is not a label without an enforceable referent; the wake is attributable to the recorded note, limited to the addressed seat, and grants no access beyond its existing evidence boundary.
- **S5 — replace with a distinct act:** COMPLETION_CLAIMED claims completion of work; the orchestrator's readiness declaration is a coordination decision, a different kind of act. Overloading one type makes the distinction depend on payload shape and actor, and the existing binding could treat a part claim as the run declaration. Recommended: **RUN_READY_FOR_COMPLETION** (orchestrator); the completion gate answers that declaration; the crew's claim remains the claim. The record then holds two honest things: the crew claims its work complete; the orchestrator declares the run ready for completion examination.
- **S6 approved with a temporal boundary:** no doorway case requirement; visibility is right. But the uncased derivation is evaluated **at the contribution's recorded position** — a later case does not retroactively case an earlier contribution, and a contribution under an effective case keeps that provenance after supersession.

**Three refinements required before approval:** (1) bind assembly records to the exact proposal and bar stale records from supporting a re-proposal; (2) define certification coverage precisely; (3) replace S5 with the distinct readiness act answered by the completion gate. Mechanical validation stays separate from the examiner's substantive judgement; the work stays within §3.8. Her closing caution: assembly, examination, readiness, and certification must remain separate acts with separate meanings, so the orchestrator never becomes an authority that quietly certifies its own decisions.
