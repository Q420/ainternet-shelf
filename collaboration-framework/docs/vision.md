# Vision

## Problem

Multi-agent systems mostly fail at collaboration itself: agents exchange messages without changing each other's work, grade their own homework, or follow scripts so rigidly that no genuine collaboration occurs. Communication gets mistaken for collaboration; a shared transcript gets mistaken for shared work.

## What we are building

A collaborative agent framework in which live agents interact **through shared work** — their contributions changing the work and influencing one another — over a **durable record** that makes the interaction recoverable and examinable. Structure lives in the record and at live-held gates, never in a speaking script. Participation never confers authority; independent examination stays distinct from the crew's work.

## Who it serves

Ben's own work first: a crew he can commission, watch, and rule on — the pattern of Ben directing, Remmy orchestrating, and Vane examining, made durable enough to run real jobs.

## Success, in Ben's terms

A real job done by a crew inside the framework, with a record in which Ben can point to one agent's contribution changing another's work — and, through repeated jobs, a crew visibly getting better at working together, or the honest finding that it isn't.

## Out of scope

- An autonomous form that runs without the director's seat occupied.
- Reviving the parked conformance programme or the Relay 01 line (separate records, Ben's decisions).
- Anything the approved plan does not name.
