## Why

The native line's evidence is, so far, one host deep. Q1 (INDEX 353) reads:
offering the granted tools natively reliably elicits the granted-tool interface
**on the pinned model as hosted by Groq**. P1's flag measurement, the refusal
shapes, and the entire call-cap chapter are likewise Groq-shaped — the cap
chapter consumed three brief rounds on a budget that is a property of Groq's
free tier, not of the framework and not of the model.

The same pinned model (`openai/gpt-oss-120b`) is hosted on the framework's other
standing route, NVIDIA. Ben's observation, which the record supports: **the
problems this programme had on the NVIDIA route were ours, not theirs** — the
120-second deaths were our connector helper's own hard-coded timeout (attempts
3–4), the later disconnect came after that plumbing was rebuilt (attempt 5),
and the 413 that pushed the line to Groq was our double-carriage defect, since
fixed by single carriage (attempt 6 → INDEX 255). NVIDIA never refused a
request of ours on size or rate; attempt 3's packages carried far more than
Groq's free tier admits, and were carried.

If the native interface reads the same on NVIDIA's hosting, the framework gains
a second evidenced route, and the first live demonstration's route becomes a
choice between two measured hosts instead of a default set by a free tier's
meter.

## The question, and the readings stated in advance

**Question:** under the identical construction that produced Q1, does the
pinned model on the NVIDIA route return well-formed native calls to the
offered tools — and what does the route report about its own limits while
doing so?

**Construction:** the admitted native-offer pair, unchanged — the baseline
request regenerated from the evidenced1 record and verified against its
commitments (hash `72f6297d…`), the modified request differing at exactly
`["tools"]` (hash `0229b0f0…`, definitions `6f3331ac…`). The requests are
route-independent; only the transport changes.

**Trials:** five, first response only, complete raw capture under the standing
evidence discipline (durable before any interpretation; nothing dispatched,
executed, or followed up; the definitions are interface, never grants — the
INDEX 331 boundary, unchanged).

**Readings, predeclared on the Groq probe's thresholds:** at ≥ 3 of 5
*delivered* trials returning `offered_tool_call × provider_accepted`, the route
**carries Q1** — the native-interface reading extends to this host. Unoffered
attempts, malformed responses, and provider failures are classified by the
settled taxonomy, preserved whole, and read as what they are; a delivery
failure is never an observation. A split result reads `split_inconclusive`,
as before.

**Pacing:** the route's own reported limits govern. Until the route reports
otherwise, the standing discipline (strictly sequential, ≥ 65 s between
request starts, monotonic) is retained — conservative on a new host, and
relaxable only on the route's own evidence at the release examination.

## What differs, stated honestly

- **The transport.** The probe's raw call must come from the NVIDIA connector
  transport with the same evidence contract the Groq helper's bounded mode
  provides (complete raw capture, capture ceiling, phase observation). Whether
  the NVIDIA helper already offers an equivalent raw/bounded mode is a
  **build-stage verification, not an assumption of this proposal** — if it does
  not, the build includes bringing it to parity, examined as part of the build.
- **`disable_tool_validation` is not in this probe.** The Groq native-offer
  probe ran at the default, and this probe mirrors it. Whether NVIDIA's host
  honours the flag, and what its refusal shapes look like, are separate
  questions, asked only if this probe's evidence makes them live ones.
- **The route's limits are taken from its own responses.** No published figure
  is assumed in either direction; whatever the route reports (admissions,
  refusals, usage) is preserved and read as the route's own accounting.

## What this is not

Not a route switch for the framework, not a worker run, not a change to the
adapter brief (which is route-agnostic in design and proceeds on its own
track). If the route carries Q1, the first live demonstration still returns
through its own release examination (N51) — with the route then named as
Ben's choice between evidenced hosts. If it does not, that is itself the
finding, and the Groq line stands as the single evidenced route.
