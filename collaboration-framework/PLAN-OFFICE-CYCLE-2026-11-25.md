# THE PLAN — THE OFFICE CYCLE (2026-11-25)

Written under the director's ruling (INDEX 578): "No more improvising. We're going to do
this with a plan." This is paper. It authorises no build. Its path is: the director's
ruling → the examiner's examination → a build scope → the build → a proof run.

The base is the director's own sketch (clipboard, 2026-11-25 15:53 MST):

    Agent + Onboarding + Briefcase  →  OFFICE (the Task inside it)
    OFFICE → Briefcase/Files → Journal → Repeat → Summarize → Brief

Everything below is an expansion of that drawing, in its order. Where a standing built
mechanism already serves the drawing, the plan says so and keeps it. Where the drawing
corrects a built mechanism, the plan names the correction plainly.

## 1 · Assembly is not work

The office assembles the agent for the job from three inputs: the AGENT (the occupant,
its pin, its identity as declared at assembly), the ONBOARDING (who he is, the role, the
role memory — the posters on the office wall), and the BRIEFCASE (the case: the task, the
evidence boundary, the delivery terms, the files stocked as blocks — §3). The TASK lives
inside the office. None of this is a step inside the job: there are no onboarding stages,
no acknowledge wakes, no ceremonies. When the job starts, the agent is already in his
office, and his first act is work.

What the agent receives at the start is ONE opening package, and §6 budgets it: his
identity and role memory framed as his own knowledge, the task, the cycle poster (this
drawing, as his map of the job), the files map in blocks with an access way per block,
the journal's address and contract (§4), and his position: the first cycle, the first
block. The tool catalogue does not ride the opening as reading matter — §5.

## 2 · The cycle is the job's shape

The job runs as a repeating cycle, once per block:

    pull one block → read it → append his bits to the journal → ◆ gate → next block

- A cycle completes when BOTH stand in the record: the block's read stands carried, and
  a journal append stands after it. Completion opens the next cycle. This is the step
  gate of INDEX 576 restated in the sketch's terms; the gate mechanism carries over.
- The gate allows or corrects (INDEX 475): if his calls run ahead — a later block read
  while an earlier cycle's journal append is missing — nothing is refused and nothing is
  lost; every call stands executed in the record, and the next package tells him, in the
  gate's shape (what arrived, what is needed, an example), which cycle he is actually on.
  Presentation walks the cycle one block at a time; his work is never refused for pacing.
- When every block's cycle stands complete, the job leaves the loop: SUMMARIZE — he
  reads his journal whole (§4) and compiles the brief from his own entries — then BRIEF:
  he contributes it, the door checks the standing gates (the evidence the case declares
  stands gathered, block by block counting as whole files; the length floor stands), and
  the office files it. The delivery machinery of INDEX 526/555/558 carries over unchanged.
- The cycle scales: five blocks or fifty, the shape does not change and the job's
  description does not grow. The tree of INDEX 554/576 remains in the record as the
  design this plan's cycle was drawn from; the packages present the CYCLE (his position
  in it), not a tree of named steps.

## 3 · Travel blocks — the unit that moves

The job's information is portioned into BLOCKS. A block is a bounded unit of material
with a shelf address: a whole small file, or one range of a large file. These are travel
blocks — the material in motion — and are not the librarian's memory blocks
(INDEX 493–496), which are how the role's memory is stored. The two never merge.

- **Stocking is the office's act.** When the briefcase is stocked, the office portions
  the files into blocks and seals the block map in the case: each block with its file,
  its line range where it is a range, its size, and its access way (the granted call
  that pulls exactly that block). The worker never decides block boundaries and never
  faces an unportioned file.
- **The standing job, portioned:** a3-pass.md = 1 block (4,663 B); stock-take.md =
  1 block (11,946 B); index-tail.md = 4 blocks (~10,000 B each, by lines, 40,953 B in
  all). Five blocks, five cycles.
- **Block ceiling (plan figure, the director's to rule): 12,000 bytes of content per
  block** — set from what the road has repeatedly accepted and from index-tail's
  quartering, with stock-take's 11,946 B standing as the largest single-file block.
  A file over the ceiling is portioned by lines into blocks under it.
- **One block in front of him at a time.** A block's content is presented once, in the
  package that follows its pull. In every later package it stands as a pointer (address
  + fingerprint). If he needs its exact words again, he pulls that one block again —
  a pull is cheap; a carriage is not.

## 4 · The journal — read + append, never carried

The journal contract as corrected by the director (INDEX 578/579) and accepted by the
examiner (INDEX 580):

- The journal is a FILE IN HIS ROOM, at a designated address in his scratch. It is not
  part of any package: the office never presents it, never carries it, never excerpts it.
- **READ:** he reads it freely, at any point — reading it is how he knows what he has
  already put there, where he stands, and what remains.
- **APPEND:** he adds his entries — per block: the block's name and the key points he
  took from it, in one or two plain lines (the shape he himself asked for, INDEX 571).
- **NO EDITING:** entries once written stand. The integrity rule the record checks:
  every new journal state has the previous state as its exact prefix — an append is the
  only legal transition. (Mechanism per lane is the build's detail: a native occupant
  appends with his shell; a text occupant's write is accepted only if the prefix rule
  holds, else it is gated with a correction, never silently filed.)
- **SUMMARIZE reads it whole.** The brief is compiled from the journal — his own bits,
  gathered one block at a time — not from the sources re-carried. If a gap shows while
  he compiles, he pulls the one block again.
- This section CORRECTS the built contracts: INDEX 508's journal carriage, carried
  through INDEX 546 and INDEX 576 (the journal presented from the record at wakes), is
  retired by this plan. The journal's designation in the case and its contract text
  carry over; its presence in packages does not.

## 5 · The catalogue lives in the office

The tool catalogue (~11,000 chars of definitions) is office stock — a poster on the
wall — not package content and not per-turn machinery.

- As PRESENTED matter it stands in the office: stocked in the room at assembly, at an
  address he can pull like any other room file. It rides no package.
- On the WIRE, the calls he may make are governed by his sealed grants, as they are
  today. The build's problem, named at INDEX 576 as a forced decision — the adapter
  built the offered definitions from the package's catalogue key, and step-only offers
  made off-step calls resolve as unknown — is answered here by design: **name
  resolution runs against the sealed catalogue in the record, not against the package.**
  The wire offers the current cycle's tools plus the journal tools (he must always be
  able to read and append his journal, at any point in the job); a call to any other
  granted tool still resolves and executes — the office never refuses his work for
  reaching — and the presentation simply notes it at the next cycle. What the wire
  offers is a courtesy; what the record resolves is the authority.

## 6 · The road — measured, then paced

The plan designs against MEASURED figures, and defers what is not yet measured.

- **Proven (INDEX 577):** on the Groq route, openai/gpt-oss-120b carries a limit of
  **8,000 tokens per minute** on this account — read from the provider's own refusal.
  The proof run died because the probe (1,820 tokens) and the opening package
  (7,530 requested) shared a minute: 9,350 > 8,000.
- **The new shape's premise (the director's, 2026-11-25 16:07 MST):** turns under this
  plan do not need anything near 8,000 tokens — a cycle's package is his position plus
  one block (≈ 12,000 B ≈ 3,000 tokens at the outside), and the opening is budgeted
  below. Pacing machinery sized for the old fat packages is not to be built for a load
  that no longer exists.
- **Per-call budget (plan figure, the director's to rule): no single request may
  exceed 4,000 tokens** — half the proven minute, so that even two calls colliding in
  one minute cannot cross the limit, and a single call always stands legal on its own.
  The opening package is designed to this budget first; if the opening cannot be
  brought under it, the plan's fallback is that the opening is the one call that goes
  out alone in its minute (the launch rule below), not a bigger budget.
- **Measured before paced:** every call's requested tokens are recorded on the wire
  (the captures already record request bytes; the build adds the provider's token
  accounting where the response carries it). The proof run's report states each call's
  figure. The pacing rule for the route is then set FROM those figures — the existing
  65-second operating pacing stands as the interim until the measurement replaces it,
  and any timer mechanism (the director's 1m10s sketch, 16:06 MST) is designed only if
  the measured figures call for it.
- **The launch seam, named:** the probe and identity calls spend from the same minute
  as the loop's calls. Whatever pacing stands must cover ALL calls on the model —
  probe included — and the run does not start its first loop call until the road's
  standing rule for the opening minute is satisfied. This is the exact seam that
  killed demo-gptoss4; the plan closes it by rule, not by hope.
- **The byte ceiling stays honestly open:** Groq has accepted requests up to ~30 KB in
  these runs and refused ~86 KB; the exact ceiling is unmeasured. Under the per-call
  token budget (≈ 16 KB at the outside) no package approaches either figure, so the
  plan does not need the byte ceiling measured to proceed — it is named here so nobody
  mistakes its absence for knowledge.

## 7 · His tools, natively

BD-1 stands corrected at the live seam (INDEX 576): the Groq transport forwards the
staged flag and offers his sealed tools; his seat is judged native. What remains
unproven is the provider's LIVE acceptance — demo-gptoss4 never reached a call. This
plan's proof run is the witness, sequenced so the road cannot kill it first: small
calls, the launch rule honoured, his first response is the acceptance test — a native
call that dispatches under his grants, or the provider's refusal recorded whole.
Ungranted calls remain refused at the doorway (R44), recorded as rejections. There is
no silent text fallback; if the provider refuses the native shape, that is the run's
result, reported as it stands, and the TOOL_SHAPE_GATE's correction path (INDEX 573)
is the designed answer for a text-judged seat, not a quiet downgrade for this one.

## 9 · The proof run and its acceptance

One cold run, GPT-OSS on Groq, no opening record, no conversation, no telling — the

- He completes the job beginning to end in his office: every block pulled, every
  cycle's journal append standing, the brief compiled from the journal and FILED —
  at or over the 550-word floor, covering the four points from the sources.
- The wire witnesses stand in the report: his native calls dispatched (BD-1's live

- Non-completion, if it comes, is reported first and plainly, with the record's own
  evidence for where the job stopped.
