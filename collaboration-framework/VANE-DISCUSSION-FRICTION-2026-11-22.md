# Vane — Discussion: friction (2026-11-22)

Her taxonomy of friction in a first live run:

- **Productive friction — the framework working.**
  Resistance that makes a real condition visible: a
  challenge naming a specific contradiction; a gate refusing
  because a recorded prerequisite genuinely isn't met;
  dissent considered, decided, and left visible; a
  correction preserving both versions and the reason; a
  checker divergence that is real. Identifiable cause,
  attributable source, a path to resolution or an explicit
  unresolved state.
- **Mechanical friction — the framework failing.** The
  machinery creates an obstacle corresponding to no real
  condition: a gate evaluating the wrong state, a wake
  missing its seat or arriving without a recorded reason, a
  classification route rejecting valid work, an observer
  presenting an absence as fact beyond its observed
  boundary, a checker divergence caused by its own defect.
  **A refusal is not proof of correctness** — the framework
  must account for its own refusals as carefully as for
  accepted acts.
- **Ambiguous friction — the framework asking us to
  investigate.** Probably most of the attention. Not
  classified by feel or by slowness, but by five questions:
  what exact condition produced it; was that condition
  actually present in the recorded work; was the rule
  authorised and correctly applied; did the machinery
  preserve enough evidence to distinguish the possibilities;
  what changed as a result. Cause and consequence are judged
  separately — a wrong challenge can still be productive; a
  correct refusal can still expose a design weakness.

Standing principles she adds: **successful collaboration is
not frictionless execution** — smoothness and difficulty are
observations, not verdicts. The classifications are not all
decided in advance; the first run must be allowed to
surprise. Where the two examiners disagree on a
classification, neither settles it by asserting superior
understanding: identify the evidence, state the competing
explanations, leave the disagreement visible for Ben.

Her proposed standard: **the framework is working when it
makes the real reasons for resistance visible and preserves
the means to examine them; it is failing when its own
machinery obscures, invents, or misrepresents those
reasons.** And the first genuinely useful discovery may be
a friction nobody has named yet.
