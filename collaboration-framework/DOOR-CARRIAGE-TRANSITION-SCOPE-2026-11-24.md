# Door, carriage, transition — design scope
*2026-11-24, Remmy, under Ben's rulings INDEX 518–520 and his synthesis as accepted by Vane (INDEX 522). Scope for examination — no build authorised by this note alone.*

The evidence, in one paragraph: run 6 proved composition inside the loop and failed delivery (INDEX 517); the Gary conversation proved delivery outside the loop and involved no room (INDEX 521). The next design joins the halves in the room, keeping Vane's separation: three parts, designed together, witnessed separately.

## Part 1 — The contribute door inside the room

When the case's assignment declares delivery-by-contribution, a contribute outcome carrying the deliverable is accepted as its arrival. The office files the contributed content at the declared destination (for this assignment, `scratch/brief.md`) as an office act, and the loop completes on the filing.

Record discipline (from INDEX 522 §4): the record distinguishes the worker's authorship from the office's filing; the file is labelled office-filed; nothing implies the worker used `sandbox.write`. Where an assignment does not declare the route, contributions remain ordinary outcomes, not deliverables.

Open points for examination:
- **Declaration shape.** A delivery declaration in the case content, beside `onboarding`: destination path + that contribution is the accepted route. Sealed at assembly; the checker honours the declaration it finds, as with the freshness suspension.
- **Amendment.** One contribution per deliverable, or a later contribution supersedes? Proposed: the first accepted contribution completes the assignment; anything after is an ordinary outcome. (Multi-draft work is a later design.)
- **The envelope problem.** Run 6's contribution never reached adjudication — his hand-written JSON envelope broke one bracket from its end. A door that dies on envelope syntax repeats the wall in a new place. Proposed path, under Ben's gate ruling (INDEX 475): when a reply is contribution-shaped but unparseable, the loop answers with the correction (what arrived, what failed, resend plainly) and the worker resubmits within the step budget — a gate, not a stall. The exact leniency boundary is the examiner's to set: the door must not become a general bypass of the outcome contract.

## Part 2 — Calibration carriage (the librarian's minimal first shape)

A `role_memory` case content field, carried at assembly from the role's standing calibration record, presented in the onboarding/cover of the first working package.

Content discipline (from INDEX 522 §2): each item carries the observation, its basis class — **demonstrated / worker-reported / inference** — and the conditions it was observed under. For Gary's role today that is, honestly: plain-prose delivery format (demonstrated, this checkpoint, conversation lane); contribution as the preferred delivery route (demonstrated, runs 5–6 + try-out); sources wanted in one labelled batch, style preferences at the outset, a bounded word count (worker-reported, his closing suggestions); the journal's invisibility without a purpose statement (demonstrated, run 5 debrief).

Bounds: the field grants nothing, alters no grants, and cannot make a preference mandatory — it informs the office's presentation and the opening conversation. The checker verifies the field travels as declared. The librarian as a maintaining agent is the later experiment (INDEX 501); this is carriage only — the summary exists because the office writes it from the record, as the office builds every briefcase.

## Part 3 — The explicit transition

The opening conversation's settled results become recorded work state: an `opening` record in the case — the assignment as the worker understood it, his questions and the office's answers, the format and style decisions taken. The first working package carries it. No private conversational context is relied on to survive the move into the room (INDEX 522 §5).

For the next run, the opening is a genuinely responsive conversation (the try-out's scripted side is the known gap, INDEX 521–522): held in the consultation lane, its settled results transcribed into the case by the office. Whether the opening later moves inside the framework's own machinery is a design question for after this run, not before it.

## The run this scope serves

Run 7, same assignment, same pinned checkpoint: opening conversation (responsive) → its record in the case → Gary works in his room with the contribute door declared open, the calibration carried, the journal labelled — and delivers. Success is stated narrowly: the brief he contributes is filed by the office as the deliverable, and the record shows each act as its own.

## What this scope is not

Not a conversational wrapper around the environment. Not the librarian agent. Not the collaboration test (INDEX 480) — that still waits on the shared workshop design. Not a change to any other population: undeclared cases behave byte-identically, as with every declaration-governed piece before this one.
