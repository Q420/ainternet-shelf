plain-words account of what the framework is, what stands
proven, what stands unproven and open, and the honest state of
the work — drawn only from the frozen evidence below, in four

cited entry; the office verifies the citations against the

## 3. The chain's mechanics (standing surfaces only, INDEX 634)

- **Unit:** a chain of bounded commissions under one case,
  conducted by the office on the standing harness. No new
  machinery, no chain-level record object. The chain's linkage
  is the shared case identity across sittings, the journal of
  record carried verbatim into each next sitting's room, and
  this scope's INDEX entries, one per sitting. Any inability
  of the existing records to show that linkage is recorded as
  a gap (INDEX 634, clarification 1), not patched mid-chain.
- **Sitting 1:** a fresh Gary instance; the whole job stated
  up front in the opening (he sees the entire volume's shape,
  works the first part); the evidence stocked; an empty
  journal. He gathers and journals, and composes into the
  journal as he goes.
- **The designed incomplete sitting:** one sitting — the
  second, unless the first ends incomplete on its own — is
  commissioned with bounds set below its portion's floor, so
  it ends mid-work with its state yielded and honestly
  incomplete in the record. Designed, and recorded as designed
  (INDEX 634, clarification 4): recovery is the claim under
  test, so the interruption is part of the experiment, not an
  accident hoped for.
- **Resumption:** the next sitting is a FRESH instance in the
  same seat (same pin, same capabilities; INDEX 634,
  clarification 3). The office stocks the same frozen evidence
  and the journal of record as the room's journal; the package
  opens with the next-step instruction (the standing worker-
  package rule) and nothing else by way of re-explanation. His
  first acts — journal read, correct next act — are the
  recovery evidence.
- **Delivery:** at the final sitting the volume is contributed
  through the standing door (contribution-v1) and filed by the
  office as the case's deliverable.
- **Per-sitting bounds:** the standing ceilings (20 steps),
  the standing Nemotron road seal, deadline 1,200 s. Chain
  length is set by the job: sittings continue until the volume
  is delivered or the director stops the chain.
