This revision incorporates all of them as settlements in the body. No code before her approval of this revision.

## Settled architecture

- **S1 — ASSEMBLY_PROPOSED (orchestrator).** Carries: `commission_seq` (the exact RUN_COMMISSIONED event it answers), `tree_ref` and `gates_ref` (the commission event's position — the proposal adopts the commissioned tree and gate declaration by reference, never copies them), and `crew` (the full proposed crew: seat + role). The doorway verifies the references resolve to the standing commission; a proposal answering a superseded commission is refused. Re-proposals are new acts; nothing is edited.
- **S2 — ASSEMBLY_RECORD (examiner).** Carries: `proposal_seq` (the exact ASSEMBLY_PROPOSED it examines — a record bound to any other proposal supports nothing), `criteria_mapping` (every proposed tree part → the commission acceptance criterion it answers), `gate_set` (the set actually examined: joints + kinds), `identity_weighed` (every proposed seat → the status weighed, from the onboarding fold). Doorway enforcement is mechanical only: the mapping covers every part of the referenced tree; the identity assessment covers every proposed seat and matches the derived statuses; the record's gate set itself satisfies the §3.7 completeness rule over the referenced tree. Whether the mapping is *right* remains the examiner's judgement. **An assembly `allow` requires, in order: a standing proposal, then a standing assembly record bound to that exact proposal.** A new proposal voids every earlier record's support — the allow's binding is to the *latest* proposal and a record that follows it.
  - **Named consequence, for her confirmation:** Ben's ruling (2026-11-21) is that gate granularity is the examiner's to approve at assembly, and her S2 settlement makes the record's gate set "the set actually examined," which may differ from the proposal's declaration. The revision therefore derives the run's **operative** gate set from the standing assembly record once one stands (validation and passage follow the approved set); the commission's declaration remains in history as the proposed set. Before any record stands, the declared set governs (the gates piece's behaviour, unchanged).
- **S3 — Certification coverage, defined.** A certification **declares its coverage**: its payload carries `covers_parts` — either absent (the whole run) or a list of tree parts. The derivation computes, within that declared coverage, every seat that recorded a CONTRIBUTION, with its identity status from the onboarding fold (no record → `unverified`). The doorway refuses a CERTIFICATION whose `identity_status` omits a covered contributor, misstates a derived status, or names a status for a seat outside the derived coverage (an invented status). The statuses ride in the certification event itself (R36).
- **S4 — Notes, routing, wake.** An orchestrator COMMENT with `to_seat` is a note; it still carries an R10 subject (a contribution or the job) — addressing creates no free-floating channel. A note may carry `routes_disagreement` (a disagreement_id) and `to_gate` (a joint): the doorway validates both referents — the disagreement stands recorded in the fold, and the joint is a gate of the operative set (or assembly/completion). The fold derives `state["routing"]`: per disagreement, the joint it was routed to and the note's position; the observer renders from that derivation (§3.9). The driver gains the deferred wake: a seat wakes on a note addressed to it, the wake attributable to the note's event; no other seat wakes, and the woken seat's view keeps its existing evidence boundary.
- **S5 — RUN_READY_FOR_COMPLETION (orchestrator), replacing the overloaded claim.** A distinct act: the orchestrator declares the run ready for completion examination. **The completion gate answers this declaration**: `completion_ready` rebinds — CERTIFICATION and RUN_CLOSED require a standing RUN_READY_FOR_COMPLETION (the latest by position) and a completion `allow` recorded after it. The crew's COMPLETION_CLAIMED keeps its exact claim semantics (a claim about completed work, folded as today, answered by nothing mechanically); the completion entry in `awaiting_gate` derives from the readiness standing. *Migration named plainly: the gates piece's T-suite armed the completion gate with claims; the build updates those witnesses to the readiness act and adds one proving a crew claim alone no longer arms the gate — the honest record Vane asked for: the crew claims, the orchestrator declares ready, the gate answers the declaration.*
- **S6 — Uncased derivation, position-sensitive.** The fold evaluates each CONTRIBUTION at its own recorded position: whether an effective case for (its seat, its part) stood *at that position* (issued with prerequisites standing, not superseded as of that position). The per-part `uncased` derivation lists the contributions recorded with no effective case standing then; later issuance never recases them, and supersession never uncases a contribution made under a standing case. No doorway requirement is added.

## Acceptance sketch (revised)

1. An assembly `allow` with no proposal, or a proposal with no record bound to it, is refused; proposal → record → allow records.
2. After an assembly `correct` and a re-proposal, the earlier record cannot support an allow; a fresh record bound to the new proposal can.
3. A record whose mapping omits a tree part, whose identity assessment omits a proposed seat or misstates a derived status, or whose gate set fails the completeness rule, is refused.
4. Where the record's gate set differs from the declaration, passage follows the record's set once it stands.
5. A certification declaring part coverage: contributors inside it must all appear with derived statuses; a contributor outside the declared parts is not required; whole-run coverage requires every contributor.
6. A certification omitting, misstating, or inventing a status is refused; an `unverified` contributor appears as such in the certification.
7. A routing note naming an unrecorded disagreement or a non-gate joint is refused; a valid one derives into `state["routing"]`.
8. An orchestrator note wakes only the addressed seat's driver.
9. A crew COMPLETION_CLAIMED alone does not arm the completion gate; RUN_READY_FOR_COMPLETION plus a following `allow` permits CERTIFICATION and RUN_CLOSED; an `allow` recorded before the latest readiness declaration does not.
10. A contribution recorded before its case issued derives uncased forever; one recorded under a standing case never derives uncased, even after supersession.
11. Regressions: all prior suites (gates witnesses migrated per S5), substrate byte-identity, prototype smoke, observer safety.
