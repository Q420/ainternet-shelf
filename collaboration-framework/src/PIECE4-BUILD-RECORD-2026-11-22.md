## What was built

- **Vocabulary + fold (record.py).** New event types: `CASE_VERIFIED`, `CASE_JUDGED`, `CASE_NOTIFIED`, `CASE_ACKNOWLEDGED`, `DRIVER_FAILURE`. The fold tracks case acts per version (verified / judged / notified / issued / acknowledged, content, corrected + representational classification) and never infers one act from another. `version_issued()` decides a version's route from its recorded classification; `effective_case()` returns the seat's highest version that completed its route. A corrected version's content stands on its CASE_CORRECTED event — authorisation never overwrites it. DRIVER_FAILURE marks the seat in a new `stalled` fold state (R33); it computes no pause.
- **Doorway.** Matrix rows for the four case acts (orchestrator / examiner / examiner / crew). `record_driver_failure()` appends DRIVER_FAILURE under the doorway's own authorship on the driver's report, on the REJECTION pattern.
- **`src/framework/driver.py`.** A `Driver` (doorway, seat, token, model callable). A wake cycle: refuse a stalled seat; take the effective case (none → no wake); compute wake reasons from the record against the seat's last act — assignment (the seat has not acted since this version issued), a contribution on its case part, a comment on its contribution, a gate outcome touching its part, a disagreement on its claim, a case revision awaiting acknowledgement; render the seat's bubble view, narrowed by the case's recorded evidence boundary; call the model with {case, view, prompt, position} — never the token; submit proposals through the doorway at the view position, stamping case provenance; on an R31 refusal, re-render. On a corrected unacknowledged version, acknowledgement proposals are submitted first and everything else held until one stands — and the remaining acts go from a re-rendered view, since the acknowledgement itself changes the case's record (found in testing: without the re-render, the doorway's staleness check rightly refused the seat's next act as made from a superseded view of its own case). Model exceptions → `provider_error`; structurally invalid returns → `malformed_output`; both record DRIVER_FAILURE and fabricate nothing.
- **Bubble.** The view's current state now renders stalled seats by kind, part, and event (R33's named state, on the working surface).

## Correction round 1 (2026-11-22, on Vane's examination)

Four provenance points, all upheld and fixed:

1. **R31: the acknowledgement path laundered stale proposals.** The first build recorded the ack, re-rendered, and submitted the SAME proposals under the new position — claiming a basis the model never saw. Fixed by splitting the acts into **two model interactions**: from the first package only the acknowledgement is recorded and everything else is held (never carried over); a second, fresh package at the new position is issued, and only proposals returned from it proceed. The suite proves it: the first package's contribution never reaches the record; the recorded contribution is the second package's, and the model demonstrably received a package at a strictly newer position.
2. **Issuance order.** `version_issued` now compares event positions: verification and judgement/notification must **precede** the authorising CASE_ISSUED; a later-arriving prerequisite does not validate an early authorisation (a fresh authorisation after the prerequisites does). Tested both ways.
3. **A corrected version needs its correction act.** A version above the initial version with no CASE_CORRECTED never issues, whatever else stands. Tested (version 6: full three acts, no correction — the standing case is untouched).
4. **Acknowledgement must postdate issuance.** The fold counts an acknowledgement only when the assigned seat recorded it after that version's authorisation; a pre-issue acknowledgement is recorded but does not satisfy R32, and the driver still holds work until a fresh one stands. Tested both ways.

Suite now **31 checks, ALL PASS**; regressions unchanged (Piece 2: 73, Piece 3: 41, ALL PASS; substrate byte-identical).

## Correction round 2 (2026-11-22, on Vane's re-examination)

One further integrity point, upheld: **CASE_CORRECTED could rewrite an already-issued version** — the fold applied every correction event unconditionally, so altered content could stand as issued without ever passing the route. Fixed in the fold (so replay preserves it, not doorway politeness): a version's correction is established **once**, by its first CASE_CORRECTED (`corrected_seq` recorded); a second correction event for the same version stands in the history but never replaces the established content or classification. `version_issued` additionally requires the correction to **precede** the whole route — verification, the examiner's act, and authorisation all act on the content the correction established. Once issued, a version is fixed; a change is a new version with its own route. Her prescribed test stands in the suite (issue v7, append a rewriting correction, the effective case cannot change), plus the ordering combination (a correction recorded after its version's verification does not issue). Suite now **33 checks, ALL PASS**; regressions unchanged. On her round-2 re-examination (implementation accepted), one test-completeness addition at her request: the rewriting test now also asserts against the replayed fold that version 7's `representational` classification remains false against the flip attempt — the two-part property (content + classification) demonstrated explicitly. Suite **34 checks, ALL PASS**.

## Stated limits

The model is a callable — no provider adapter, no live-model claim, and the §7 "two live seats" acceptance remains outstanding until an adapter and the §3.5 office exist. Timeouts are not separately handled: a synchronous callable cannot be interrupted in-process; provider adapters will carry real timeouts. A wake cycle whose model returns no acts leaves its reasons standing for the next invoked cycle — with no scheduler (R29) nothing spins on them, and the baseline moves only on recorded acts. Orchestrator notes (R11) await §3.8's recorded form; the driver consumes no substitute. The case content's field names (`part`, `evidence_boundary`, …) are as recorded in the case payload; §3.5 will fix the canonical R14 shape.
