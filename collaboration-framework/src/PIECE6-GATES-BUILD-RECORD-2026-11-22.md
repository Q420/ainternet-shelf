## What was built

- **`src/framework/gates.py` (new)** — declaration validation and gate queries over the fold. `validate_gate_set` implements the completeness rule (roots = parts that are no part's downstream; every non-root part a required joint; exactly one assembly and one completion gate; no judgement gate naming a root or a non-tree part; every defect named by part and fault). `enrich(state)` derives `state["gate_set"] = {declared, valid, defects}` and `state["waiting"]` as pure functions of the folded record — derivation is a post-fold step, never a recorded act, so replay reproduces it exactly. Waiting derives from **whether passage is permitted**, not from whether an outcome exists: a gate holding on `correct` still awaits; an unruled escalation shows `awaiting_ruling`, distinctly. Queries: `assembly_open` (valid declaration **and** standing assembly `allow` — the one release condition, fail-closed), `passage_blocker` (a judgement gate holds its part and its downstream until `allow`), `resolve_part` (every work act's part from its recorded subject — a part, the job, or unresolvable), `completion_ready` (claim standing **and** an `allow` recorded after it, by position), `latest_ruling`.
- **`record.py` (fold, additive only)** — the commission's `gates` map carried verbatim as `state["gate_declaration"]`; `HOLDER_UNAVAILABLE` added to the vocabulary (one type, deliberately, per G6 as settled) and folded as declarations; RULING entries now carry their `joint` and `action`; COMPLETION_CLAIMED sets `completion_claim_seq`; REJECTION entries carry `part`.
- **`doorway.py`** — `ACT_AUTHORITY` gains `HOLDER_UNAVAILABLE` (orchestrator). `state()` returns the enriched fold. `_gate_checks` enforces, for declared runs only (G7): **work acts** — the pre-run block (every work act and CASE_ACKNOWLEDGED refused until the run is open; issuance is gate-process and unblocked, G2) and passage (part resolved from the subject; unresolvable refused fail-closed and recorded; a resolved part outside the recorded tree refused under R21); **GATE_OUTCOME** — the ruling mechanics (an `allow` refused while the latest ruling stands at `stop that part`; an `allow` refused over a standing escalation with no director ruling against the same gate — a ruling permits the examiner's allow, never manufactures one; a re-judgement after `correct as stated` must carry `correction_basis` naming recorded acts that postdate the ruling and precede the outcome — existence and sequence verified mechanically, sufficiency left to the examiner); **CERTIFICATION / RUN_CLOSED** — refused unless `completion_ready`. `record_blocked_wake(seat, part)` records a pre-assembly wake attempt as a doorway REJECTION naming seat, part, and the R22 ground (G5) — never a DRIVER_FAILURE. One further doorway change, made for the owed onboarding regression: a refusal on a token the doorway issued but which no longer authenticates (the expired commission token) now names that token's seat in the recorded REJECTION, via `_presented_seat`; a token never issued still names no seat.
- **`driver.py`** — the wake cycle folds the enriched state and checks the assembly state **before** invoking the model: on a declared run that is not open, a wake that was actually going to happen (effective case + recorded reasons) is recorded through `record_blocked_wake` and the model is never called.
